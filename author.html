The Story behind The Book

In my early twenties, I went through a personal betrayal that left me reeling for years. A close friend — someone I trusted deeply — was secretly bad-mouthing me to others while sabotaging a project we were working on together and stealing money from me. When I found out, I was devastated. Anger and hurt swirled inside me. I thought, “How could he do this? He ruined everything!” In my mind, I labeled him as “villain” and myself as “victim”, and I started rewriting the story of my life with his betrayal as a central chapter. Perhaps you know the script: I’m this way because so-and-so hurt me. It’s an easy narrative to adopt when we’re in pain.

For a long time, I clung to that narrative. It absolved me of looking at my own mistakes; (I made a few in that project too) and it gave me a clear target to blame for my struggles. But, carrying that story was heavy and disempowering. I felt like I was walking around with an arrow sticking out of my back, constantly reminding me of how I’d been wronged.

It took a lot of time and inner wrestling to realize a diﬃcult truth. After replaying the betrayal in my mind for what felt like the thousandth time, I caught myself mid-rumination and asked: Who’s writing the story of my life right now? Them… or G-d? Or me?”

This isn’t a book of theory. It’s a real-life experiment: “What would happen if I let everything go?” Rabbi Bernath shares himself, and some of his most painful private experiences, throughout the course of the book. Most importantly, he shares how the experiment changed his life in ways he couldn’t predict or understand.. at first.

It’s a work inseparable from its author, like a partial autobiography. Want to get to know the rabbi better? Listen to his podcasts or visit his social media.

More About the Author

SPIRITUAL LEADER & FAMILY MAN

Rabbi Yisroel Bernath is the spiritual director at Chabad of NDG and the Jewish Chaplain at Concordia University. He received his BA and Rabbinical Ordination from the Central Yeshivas Tomchei Temimim in Brooklyn, NY, and his MA in Hebrew Letters from the Yeshiva Gedolah Rabbinical College of Greater Miami. He also has a diploma from Hadassah-Wizo for training in Structural Cognitive Modifiability. He has extensive experience working in Adult Education and Special Education as well as coaching hundreds of young adults and couples.

In 2010, he was Canada's only finalist for the Jewish Community Hero Award. Known as “The Love Rabbi,” he exudes warmth and has an air of non-judgmental approachability. It’s no wonder he’s one of Canada's most popular under-40 rabbis! Rabbi Bernath lives in Montreal's trendy Monkland Village with his beloved wife Sara and adorable children Chaya, Zalmy, Leiba, Hena, and Yair.

DATING COACH & MATCHMAKER

Having made over 200 matches to date, Rabbi Bernath is lovingly called “The Love Rabbi” - and for good reason! In 2012, he founded the smashing success JMatchmaking International, a network of Jewish dating sites. In 2017, he starred in the hit CBC Documentary "Kosher Love," which was nominated for a Canadian Screen Award and produced and directed by Academy Award winners Frederic Bohbot and Evan Beloff.

Rabbi Bernath is a legend on social media. Boasting tens of thousands of dedicated followers, Rabbi Bernath posts amazing insights on both love and marriage, and Judaism as a whole. He also hosts frequent Facebook and Instagram Lives, where he provides advice that goes to the core of your love questions.

FILM & TELEVISION STAR

Beyond being at the centre of CBC's hit documentary "Kosher Love", Rabbi Bernath is by all definitions an innovative and versatile media mogul. As a professional voice-over artist and storyteller, he has lent his voice to hundreds of productions and performed all over the world. As a screenwriter, Rabbi Bernath co-wrote the award-winning full feature animation "Young Abraham," and as an actor, he performed in The Will (2010) and Mixed Blessings (2012). His other credits include work with Shazak Inc, Sandy Productions, CMJ Productions, Big Bang Animation, and Realtime Jewish Media. In 2016, Rabbi Bernath starred in Viceland's popular show Dead Set on Life where he lovingly showed Chef Matty Matheson around as they navigated the exciting world of Kosher Montreal. Rabbi Bernath is also a frequent guest on CTV’s Your Morning, Breakfast TV Montreal, and Global News Montreal.
