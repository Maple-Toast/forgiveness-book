What to Expect

Rabbi Bernath has flown around the world to give keynotes, be a scholar-in-residence, run retreats and Shabbatons, and more. He brings warmth, a knack for storytelling, and the ability to bring new people into your community organization.

Known for his ability to connect emotionally with audiences, Rabbi Bernath also spends time getting to know people and chatting 1-on-1. Far from being an aloof visitor, he’s a friendly and down-to-earth presence that your organization and community will remember fondly for years to come.
